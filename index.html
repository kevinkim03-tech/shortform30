How it works

From kickoff to posted

The same four steps every month, so you always know what's coming and when.

  1. STEP 1 · DAY 1

    Kickoff call

    We learn your offer, your audience and your voice in a 30-minute call. Send us any footage, photos or past posts you already have.

  2. STEP 2 · DAYS 2–5

    Hooks and scripts

    We write 30 scripts, each with a hook built for the first three seconds. You approve the list before we cut anything.

  3. STEP 3 · DAYS 6–12

    Edit and caption

    Every video gets cut to under 30 seconds, captioned, sized for each platform and kept inside the on-screen safe zones.

  4. STEP 4 · DAY 13+

    Post and repeat

    You get a ready-to-post calendar with captions and hashtags. At month end we review what worked and plan the next 30.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to film anything myself?

Not necessarily. We can build videos from footage, photos and product shots you already have. If you'd like to appear on camera, we'll send you a short shot list you can film on your phone in under an hour.

Why under 30 seconds?

Short videos are more likely to be watched all the way through, and completion is one of the strongest signals the platforms use to decide what to show more people.

Can I request changes?

Yes. You approve scripts before we edit, and every video includes a round of revisions before it lands in your calendar.

Do you post for us?

By default we deliver a ready-to-post calendar. If you'd rather we schedule posts directly to your accounts, ask about it on your kickoff call.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans run month to month, and you can cancel before your next kickoff.
